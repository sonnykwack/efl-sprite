# Held-out evaluation set (ES-1)

Written 2026-11-04 for the teacher-vs-AI agreement study. None of these texts were used to
design prompts or rules (unlike AD-1). The teacher scores every answer BLIND (before seeing
the app's result), then enters the band on the result page; History shows MAE / within-0.5 /
bias.

The tasks and answers were drafted with the AI coding tool at deliberately different levels
(the author specified the levels and error types to cover), entered in the app, and then
scored BLIND by the author, a working TOEFL instructor. Intended levels (design target,
NOT ground truth — the teacher's band is):

| Task | Answer | Intended | Designed features |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email 1 — Missing the study group | A | ~2.5 | all 3 points but thin; "Because" fragment, "change"→changed, "in home", "until"→by, "nobody do" |
| Email 1 | B | ~4.5–5 | all points developed, natural register, few/no errors |
| Email 2 — Early-morning construction | A | ~3.5 | all points, polite register; "there is"→has been, "a construction", "my study", "in my floor", "same problem" |
| Email 2 | B | ~2 | very short, demanding/impolite register, points thin, many errors |
| AD 2 — Free public transportation | A | ~4.5–5 | engages both posts, new argument, concrete example, wide range |
| AD 2 | B | ~3 | new reason + family example; agreement errors, "not big problem", sentence-initial So/But |
| AD 2 | C | ~2 | repeats Jake, no example, "I am disagree", fragment, "many money" |
| AD 3 — Mandatory volunteer work | A | ~4–4.5 | nuanced position, school example, alternative proposal |
| AD 3 | B | ~2.5–3 | repeats Sofia, nursing-home example; fragment, "did volunteer", "help", "for graduate" |

Speaking tasks (Interview 1–2, Repeat 1) are registered for the recording feature.
